INSTITUTIONAL GOVERNANCE

Legal, Privacy & Cross-Border Compliance

Effective Date: November 2026 • Applicable across UAE, United Kingdom, United States, and European Union B2B engagements.

1. Privacy Policy (GDPR & CCPA Aligned)

Pravah International (“we”, “our”, “the Firm”) processes commercial contact and fleet telemetry strictly on a need-to-know basis for evaluating, deploying, and maintaining automated intake systems.

1.1 Data Collected

  • Pilot Intake Submissions: Operator Name, Fleet/Company Name, Fleet Size, WhatsApp Business/Direct Number, and Operating Hub.
  • Document Triage Pipelines: Client Passport and International Driving Permit (IDP) images processed in production are routed exclusively through client-owned encrypted endpoints with zero unauthorized third-party resale.
  • Local Preferences: Cookie consent status stored locally in your browser (localStorage) without invasive cross-site tracking pixels.

1.2 Data Subject Rights

Authorized representatives in the EEA, UK, UAE, and California may request access, rectification, or erasure of submitted corporate contact records by contacting our compliance desk via your assigned pilot channel.

2. Terms of Service

By accessing this website or initiating a 14-day fleet pilot with Pravah International, you represent that you are an authorized representative of a commercial mobility, rental, or chauffeur enterprise.

2.1 Scope of Automation Infrastructure

  • Pravah International architects deterministic workflow pipelines (including WhatsApp Business API integrations, KYC extraction nodes, and multi-currency deposit logic) tailored to each operator’s Standard Operating Procedures.
  • All final vehicle release decisions, insurance underwriting approvals, and payment gateway captures remain under the sole authority of the client fleet operator.

2.2 Intellectual Property

All proprietary workflow topologies, prompt architectures, and interface systems remain the intellectual property of Pravah International unless explicitly assigned under a signed Master Services Agreement (MSA).

3. Cross-Border B2B Technology Consultancy & Reverse-Charge Tax Compliance

Pravah International operates as an international B2B systems consultancy serving commercial entities across the United Arab Emirates, United Kingdom, United States, and European Union.

  • Export of Services & Reverse-Charge Mechanism: Cross-border software engineering, workflow automation, and technical advisory services rendered to VAT/GST-registered business entities outside the Firm’s domestic jurisdiction are billed on a business-to-business (B2B) export-of-services basis.
  • Client Tax Responsibility: Where applicable under local statutory law (including EU VAT Directive Article 44/196, UK VAT Reverse Charge, or UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 8), the recipient commercial entity is solely responsible for accounting for any applicable reverse-charge VAT, use tax, or withholding obligations in its operating jurisdiction.
  • Currency & Invoicing: Institutional retainers and pilot deployments are invoiced in AED, USD, EUR, or GBP as specified in the governing Statement of Work (SOW).
